📜 Cooking Guide & Hearth Safety
🔥 Hearth & Steam Safety:

Boiling kettles and steamers reach over 100°C. In home cooking, never lift hot metal cookware bare-handed; always use folded kitchen towels or tongs, and cook with adult guidance.

How to Cook:

  • Cook with Tara: She shares her family's cooking notes at every step.
  • Tactile Interactions: Select ingredients from the pantry, sprinkle water into the brass bowl, alternate coconut and rice in the steamer cylinder, and plate on banana leaf.
  • Breakfast Mukbang: Click the warm food on the leaf to share bites with Chintu and watch his natural reactions.
  • Beginner Pointer: An indicator points to the next step if you're ever wondering what to do.
